§ 03 · Signal taxonomy

Six categories. Everything else is noise.

Each candidate item maps to one of these. If it doesn't fit, it doesn't ship. The kill list at the bottom is where most generic "China-MENA relations" items get buried.

A · Market entry

Chinese co. enters Gulf

Distributor deal, local office, R&D center, showroom network, pilot license, JV, free-zone setup. Priority sectors: EV, AV, robotics, AI/cloud, battery, logistics, advanced manufacturing.

B · Capital flow

Gulf capital → China

PIF, Mubadala, ADQ, ADIA, QIA, Aramco Ventures / Prosperity7, CYVN, MGX, eWTP investing in Chinese startups, funds, ETFs, manufacturing platforms.

C · Localization

Supply-chain transfer

Chinese OEM/supplier building Gulf assembly, parts, battery recycling, materials, industrial parks, data centers, logistics nodes.

D · Gov-to-gov

Framework unlocks

MOUs, joint committees, FTA progress, BIT updates, sovereign co-investment platforms, procurement frameworks that create future pipeline.

E · Proof points

Commercial reality

First operations, regulatory approvals, fleet deployments, named orders, procurement volumes, local hiring, paying Gulf customer traction.

F · People

Executive movement

Regional-head appointments, new China-Gulf-mandate funds, chambers/business forums with named delegations, key hires that signal commitment.

Kill criteria

An item is killed when ANY of these are true:

  • Generic China-MENA geopolitics with no capital, company, project, or market-access angle.
  • Energy commodity moves unless they change industrial/logistics feasibility.
  • Pure PR with no named counterpart, sector, geography, or next step.
  • Diplomatic "relations are strong" coverage not tied to deal pipeline.
  • Deals outside GCC unless clearly a China-Gulf platform / third-market strategy.
  • Consumer auto launches with no distribution / orders / fleet / localization angle.
§ 04 · Editorial filter

Five questions before a story ships.

The bar is "does this matter to Raymond specifically" — not "is this interesting." A candidate must score on at least three of the five. Two soft hits aren't enough.

  1. Does it map to the two-ecosystem thesis?

    China supply / tech meeting Gulf market / capital / regulatory sandbox. If you can't draw the corridor in one sentence, kill it.

  2. Is there a named, actionable actor?

    Company, fund, ministry, executive, chamber, free zone, distributor — someone Raymond could know, partner with, sell to, or track. "A Chinese company" is not an actor.

  3. Is it in or near a Dhow / WAG lane?

    Higher score for automotive, EV, AV, fleet/logistics, AI infra, robotics, industrial localization, venture-studio wedge ideas. Adjacent lanes get a flag, not a kill.

  4. Is this pipeline or proof?

    Signed agreement, license issued, money committed, plant location confirmed, first orders booked — beats vague intent or "studying cooperation."

  5. What changed since yesterday?

    New capital number, new license, commercial launch, facility location, procurement volume, named counterpart. If the news is "still talking," it's not news.

§ 07 · Open questions

What I need from you.

Five decisions before this ships. Pick a default for each — I'll start opinionated and you correct if wrong.

  1. Geographic scope: strictly GCC, or include Iran / Egypt / Turkey when Chinese firms use them as stepping stones to GCC?
    My default: GCC + Egypt (Egypt is a real Chinese manufacturing beachhead now). Iran and Turkey only if directly tied to a GCC angle.
  2. Auto retail granularity: showroom / distributor launches, or only fleet / procurement / local assembly?
    My default: fleet + assembly only. Showroom news is too noisy and rarely actionable for WAG.
  3. Paywalled sources (MEED, AGBI, Caixin Pro): summarize from snippets, or skip until accessible?
    My default: summarize from snippets with a "paywalled" tag — better than missing the signal.
  4. Empty-day behavior: post every day, or only on qualified-signal days?
    My default: only post if ≥2 qualified items or ≥1 hero item. Quiet days stay quiet — protects channel signal density.
  5. CRM pipeline: auto-write promising leads (named company + Gulf counterparty + suggested intro path) to a Notion / brain page?
    My default: Phase 2. Ship the daily drop first; layer CRM after we see what kind of leads it actually surfaces.
