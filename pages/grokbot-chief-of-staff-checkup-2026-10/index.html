1 · Chief of Staff

You talk to Zosia; Zosia talks to the team

Zosia owns your chat, priorities, approvals and delegation. Specialists do the domain work and report back.

Five handoff rules

  1. You talk to Zosia by default. Zosia routes by domain and sends you one consolidated answer.
  2. Specialists never message you unprompted. They deliver to Zosia, except scheduled briefs you subscribed to.
  3. Every irreversible action (send, post, book, pay) goes through a draft and your approval. Zosia holds the approval queue.
  4. One owner per routine: no duplicate prep or finance runs. Zosia keeps a routing table in its store.
  5. Every handoff carries context, the decision needed, and a deadline. Results go back to a shared store.
