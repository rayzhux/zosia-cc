Five handoff rules

  1. You talk to Zosia by default; she sends one consolidated answer.
  2. Specialists never ping you unprompted; they report into Zosia's store.
  3. Every send, post, booking or payment is a draft in the decision queue first.
  4. One owner per routine, recorded in Zosia's routing table.
  5. Every handoff carries context, the decision needed, a deadline and a done-when.

4 · Proactivity

Fewer, better touchpoints: a rhythm, triggers, and a noise budget

Built from this week's friction: prep that arrived twice, the same clash nudged repeatedly, and times shown in the wrong zone.

Friction this weekRule that prevents it
Meeting prep arrived twiceSingle owner (Intel) and a dedupe key per calendar event
The same clash was nudged again and againOne clash card; re-raise only on change or at T-2h
Meeting times shown hours offNormalize every time to Asia/Dubai and label it

Event triggers

EventWhat happens
Slack mention or DMAdd to waiting-on-me; surface in next brief unless VIP or due today
Email from a VIP listImmediate summary plus a drafted reply in the decision queue
Calendar added, moved or clashingOne clash card with a recommendation; re-raise only if it changes or at T-2h
Meeting ends (Granola / Fathom)Intel sends an action card within 15 min; promises go into the ledger
Statement, invoice or renewal mailFinance checks it; only exceptions reach you
Deadline in under 24 hEscalate now, outside the brief

Commitments and waiting-on ledger

  • Two lists: what you owe, and what others owe you
  • Each item: owner, due date (Dubai), source link, status
  • Fed by meeting cards, Slack and mail
  • Nudge once at due minus one day; overdue items go to the weekly review
Decision queue · exampleAccept the 11:00 steering meeting and end tennis at 10:45?

Clash with a court booking. Recommendation: accept; the booking can shorten. Expires at 09:00.

Anti-noise policy

  • One nudge per item per state change. Never repeat an unchanged nudge.
  • Bundle all meeting prep into the morning brief; one prep owner (Intel) with a dedupe key per event.
  • Quiet hours 21:00 to 07:00 Dubai, except escalations.
  • At most 6 unscheduled pings a day; the rest wait for the next brief.
  • If a draft already exists, point to it; never create a second.
  • Every time shown in Asia/Dubai with a label, tested in every routine.

Escalation

  • Now: deadline under 24 h, VIP sender, money or legal exposure, a booking about to lapse.
  • Next brief: everything else that needs you.
  • Weekly review: patterns, slipping commitments, idle bots.
  • Never: FYI-only items with no decision.

How specialists report

  • Write results to Zosia's store with a priority and a deadline
  • Zosia merges them into the next touchpoint
  • Only escalations bypass the rhythm, and only through Zosia
  • Scheduled briefs you subscribed to are the one exception

Memory hygiene

  • Every fact dated, with its source
  • Decisions logged once, then referenced
  • Weekly prune of stale or superseded notes
  • No secrets or credentials in memory, ever

5 · Concepts we borrow

We take the ideas from Hermes, not the code

Hermes is only a source of ideas. No Hermes skill code or prompts are reused. Each concept gets a fresh design and a security audit: inputs treated as data, read-only by default, drafts before sends.

#ConceptWhy it mattersNative Grok Bot design
1A defined persona voiceA Chief of Staff that has a take is more useful than a neutral summarizer.Write Zosia's profile from scratch: direct, recommends, switches to Chinese when you do.
2A daily intelligence rhythmPredictable touchpoints beat random pings.Intel and Zosia routines on fixed Dubai times, built on calendar, mail, Slack and notes connectors.
3Meeting cards before and afterPrep and follow-up are where meetings pay off.Intel routine triggered by calendar and meeting-end events; one card per meeting.
4Action items into a ledgerPromises get lost across mail, chat and calls.A ledger file in Zosia's store, fed by meeting notes, Slack and mail; nudge at due minus one day.
5Structured delegation briefsClear handoffs stop rework.A Zosia skill: context, decision needed, deadline, done-when; results written back to the store.
6A board of open workOne place to see what each bot is doing.Kanban list in the shared store, rendered in the weekly review.
7Self-analytics and healthYou can't tune what you don't measure.Ops routine on the box: connector status, credits, ping counts, the proactivity metrics.
8Recovering blocked pagesMany useful sites block bots.Shared skill: try fetch, then browser on the box, then the Mac mini only if a local IP is required.
9One channel per domainMirrors the org chart and keeps noise sorted.Each bot has its own chat; Zosia's chat is the only one that pings you.
