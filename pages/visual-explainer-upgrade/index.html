What to try interactive

Smart fit (↻ button): Resets to the optimal zoom. With 18 nodes, it uses width-priority mode instead of shrinking everything to fit.
1:1 button: Shows the diagram at native SVG size — readable text, you pan to navigate.
Pinch on phone: Two-finger pinch to zoom in/out, one finger to pan.
Double-click: Same as smart fit — snaps back to optimal view.
⛶ expand: Opens the diagram full-size in a new tab with matching background color.
