Technical anatomy

A join follows a calendar event, lives in the call, and ends as a summary Page

Each bot calls JoinMeeting itself: now, once at a set time, or on a recurring schedule (at most daily) in an IANA timezone. Following a calendar event by occurrence id is the sturdy option: the join moves when the event moves and stops when it is cancelled.

  1. 1Schedule now, once, or recurring (weekly weekdays, monthly by day or “last Friday”). Recurring joins follow the series RRULE.
  2. 2Links from calendars must sit on meet.google.com, zoom.us, teams.microsoft.com or teams.live.com.
  3. 3Joins under its own bot name with its avatar. Interactive answers anyone in chat; listen-only takes notes.
  4. 4Speaker-named transcript; notes hold decisions, owners, open questions, key facts. Readable mid-call.
  5. 5On end the bot writes topics, decisions and Next Steps with owners to a Library Page and sends the link.

Decision · who joins

Choose the hybrid: a Notetaker joins every call, Zosia delivers every result

The joining bot’s name and avatar are what external attendees see, and its meeting records stay with it. Those two facts decide the design.

OPTION A

Zosia joins everything

Pros

  • Works today with zero setup
  • All meeting context lands in the bot that builds your brief
  • Records and follow-ups in one place

Cons

  • Attendees see “Zosia”, which reads like an unknown person
  • Every summary link lands in Zosia’s chat
  • Meeting records mix into Zosia’s wider memory
▲ Use for the pilot
OPTION B

Dedicated bot reports to you directly

Pros

  • Neutral, professional name and avatar in every call
  • Zosia’s chat stays clean
  • Meeting records sit in one narrow bot

Cons

  • A second bot talks to you about your day
  • Brief and ledger still need a handoff
  • One more bot to maintain
■ Splits your point of contact
OPTION C · RECOMMENDED

Hybrid: Notetaker joins, Zosia delivers

Pros

  • Professional identity in every call
  • Zosia stays your single contact and keeps a clean chat
  • Records stay narrow; summaries flow to Zosia
  • One route for action items into the COO ledger

Cons

  • Needs a standing Notetaker → Zosia handoff
  • One new bot via dr eggbot
  • Mid-call questions go to the Notetaker
● Recommended
● Recommendation

Go with C, in two moves. Zosia pilots on one internal call today, since it needs no new bot. Once you approve, dr eggbot creates the Notetaker and it takes over all joins. Sensitive calls stay unjoined in every option.

New bot spec

Raymond’s Notetaker: one job, one handoff

Ready for Zosia to pass to dr eggbot once you approve step 3.

Spec · for dr eggbot
Name
Raymond’s Notetaker (team role: Chief of Meetings). The name tells every attendee what it is.
Avatar
Neutral monogram “RZ” with a pen line on navy. Camera on, showing the avatar.
Purpose
Join Raymond’s approved online calls, keep notes, write summaries, hand them to Zosia.
Owns
All meeting joins and schedules (via JoinMeeting, following calendar events); meeting records and summary Pages; the interactive or listen-only choice by attendee domain; mid-call answers.
Reads
All three calendars: work Outlook and two Google calendars.
Hands to
Zosia: summary link, decisions, action items with owners, after every call. Zosia routes to the COO and drafts follow-ups.
Stays out of
Health, hiring, HR and legal calls; email sending; gbrain writes; any channel beyond meeting chat and Zosia.

Caveats

New feature, chat-only voice, records tied to the joining bot

Launched 11 Nov 2026

One day old. Pilot on one internal call before relying on it.

Chat only

The bot speaks through meeting chat. Questions and answers happen there.

Records stay with the joiner

Pilot records stay with Zosia after the Notetaker takes over. Start fresh joins from the Notetaker.

Handoff needs your instruction

Meeting details go only to the person who asked. A standing instruction lets the Notetaker share with Zosia.

Later events need a new join

Joins cover events that exist when scheduled. Zosia’s brief flags new calls.

External posts are reviewed

In calls not marked internal, each chat post is checked first; blocked posts come to you as a draft card.
