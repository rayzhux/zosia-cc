Why SQLite over PostgreSQL?

Your Mac is a single-user system. SQLite is zero-config, zero-daemon, and I can query it directly with Python — no Docker, no connection strings. File-based = easy backup (just copy the .db file). For personal health data volume (even 10 years), SQLite handles it effortlessly. If you ever need Grafana, it has a SQLite plugin. If you need to scale later, we migrate — but you won't need to.

03 — Analysis Strategy

What to Actually Analyze (Best Practices)

Based on research across quantified-self communities, sports science literature, and Apple Health analysis projects. These are the metrics and correlations that actually produce actionable insights.

Tier 1: The Big Five — Track These Daily

Resting Heart Rate
❤️ RHR
Best single health proxy. Trend matters more than absolute number. Rising RHR = stress, illness, overtraining.
Heart Rate Variability
📈 HRV
Autonomic nervous system health. Higher = better recovery. Apple Watch measures SDNN during sleep. Most actionable metric.
Sleep Duration + Phases
😴 Sleep
Total hours + deep/REM/core breakdown. Deep sleep is recovery gold. Track consistency (same bedtime = better quality).
VO2 Max (Cardio Fitness)
🫁 VO2
Strongest predictor of longevity. Apple estimates from outdoor walks/runs. Target: "Above Average" for your age. Per Attia: top metric.
Active Energy + Steps
🔥 Activity
Daily movement baseline. 7k-10k steps is the sweet spot for mortality benefit. Track weekly trend, not daily noise.

Tier 2: Cross-Correlations — Where Real Insights Live

Individual metrics are context. Correlations between them are insights. These are the pairings that the quantified-self community and sports science have found most actionable:

CorrelationWhat It RevealsActionable Signal
Sleep duration → next-day HRV Your personal sleep-recovery curve Find your minimum sleep for good HRV (probably 7-7.5h). Below that = compromised recovery.
Exercise intensity → HRV (next morning) Recovery capacity / overtraining risk If HRV stays suppressed 48h+ post-workout → you're training too hard or not recovering enough.
Tennis sessions → RHR trend Cardiovascular adaptation to your sport RHR should gradually decrease over months of consistent training. Stalling = plateau.
Sleep consistency → HRV baseline Circadian rhythm impact Same bedtime ±30min = significantly higher average HRV. Irregular sleep crushes recovery.
Step count → sleep quality Activity-sleep feedback loop Most people sleep better on 8k+ step days. Find your personal threshold.
Resting HR trend → illness prediction Early warning system RHR spike of 5+ bpm for 2+ days often precedes getting sick by 24-48 hours.
VO2 Max trend → training effectiveness Are your workouts actually working? VO2 Max should trend up over 3-6 month blocks. Flat = need to change training stimulus.
Workout HR recovery → fitness level Post-exercise heart rate drop How fast HR drops in first 60s post-exercise. Faster recovery = fitter. Track over months.

Tier 3: Weekly Health Digest (Automated)

Every Sunday, I'll generate and post a Discord CV2 card to #health with:

Weekly Scorecard

  • Avg RHR (vs last week + 30-day trend)
  • Avg HRV (vs last week)
  • Total sleep hours + avg duration
  • Deep sleep % of total
  • Steps per day average
  • Active calories total
  • VO2 Max current reading

Insights & Flags

  • Best/worst sleep night + why (late bedtime? exercise day?)
  • HRV trends — improving, declining, or stable?
  • Workout summary — type, duration, calories
  • Tennis-specific: match count, avg duration, HR peak
  • Any anomalies flagged (unusual RHR, missing data)

Recommendations

  • Sleep consistency score + suggested bedtime
  • Recovery readiness (based on HRV + RHR)
  • Training load guidance (push harder vs. ease off)
  • Compare to your best weeks — what was different?
04 — Proactive Alerts

Real-Time Anomaly Detection

Don't wait for the weekly digest. These conditions trigger immediate alerts:

🔴 Immediate Alert

  • Resting HR spikes +8 bpm above 7-day avg
  • HRV drops >25% below baseline
  • Sleep under 5 hours for 2+ consecutive nights
  • No activity data for 24+ hours (sync failure detection)

🟡 Weekly Trend Alert

  • RHR trending up for 5+ consecutive days
  • Sleep duration declining week-over-week for 3 weeks
  • VO2 Max dropped 1+ point from last reading
  • Zero workouts logged in 7+ days
05 — Data Schema

SQLite Schema Design

TableKey ColumnsSource Metrics
sync_payloads id, received_at, metric_count Audit trail of every HAE sync
heart_rate date, min, avg, max, context, source Continuous HR + resting HR
hrv date, value_ms (SDNN), source Heart Rate Variability
sleep_analysis start, end, stage (core/deep/rem/awake), source Sleep phases + duration
workouts type, start, end, duration, calories, distance, hr_avg, hr_max All workout types including Tennis
daily_metrics date, steps, active_cal, resting_cal, distance, flights Daily activity rollups
vitals date, metric_name, value, unit VO2 Max, SpO2, respiratory rate, body temp
body date, weight_kg, bmi, body_fat_pct Weight + composition (if logged)

All tables have UNIQUE(date, source) constraints for idempotent inserts. Indexes on date columns for fast range queries.
