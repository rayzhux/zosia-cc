Problems Found

1. Channel sprawl

  • 34 channels total, only 14 active
  • 5 dead Productivity channels with 0-1 messages ever
  • 10 archived channels still visible in sidebar
  • No clear “for Raymond” vs “for the machine” boundary

2. No proactive channel

  • I can only react in #daily or post cron output to fixed channels
  • No place for me to drop briefs, flags, research, or WIP unprompted
  • Meeting briefings post to #meeting but you have to go find them

3. Intelligence scattered

  • Daily brief → #daily-news, Reddit → #reddit, X → #x-gcc — 3 channels for related intel
  • No unified “morning dashboard” view
  • You have to check 3+ channels to get the full picture

4. Stale experiments cluttering

  • #crm, #notion, #linear, #knowledge-base — experiments that didn’t stick
  • They add cognitive load when scanning the sidebar
  • Some still have open threads implying they’re active

5. Two crons broken

  • Apple Health Daily and Opik Daily both failing with “message failed”
  • Likely session/channel binding issue from recent restarts
  • Silently failing — no alert mechanism

6. No cron failure alerting

  • When a cron errors, it just increments consecutiveErrors
  • Nobody gets pinged — you find out days later (or never)
  • Should auto-alert to #system or DM on consecutive failures
