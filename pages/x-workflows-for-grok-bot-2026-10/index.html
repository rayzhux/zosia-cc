1 · How people use Grok Bot with X

Public X is built in; your own feed needs one connector.

Since 7 Oct 2026 Grok Bot can search, read and monitor public X posts natively, with no setup (TeslaNorth, AICoder). Anything tied to your account goes through the X connector.

● built in No setup

  • Search public postsnative
  • Read public posts and threadsnative
  • Monitor accounts and topicsnative

◆ connector Linked to your X account

  • Your home timelineX connector
  • Your mentionsX connector
  • Your bookmarksX connector

What it does

  • Routines run in the cloud, even with the laptop closed.
  • Drafts posts in your voice.

What it doesn’t do

  • It doesn’t post or DM by itself. You review and post.
  • Likes, replies and posts stay behind your approval.

The recommended way to build a routine

  1. Do the task once by hand.
  2. Run it twice to check links and timestamps.
  3. Save it as a routine.
  4. Keep likes, replies and posts behind approval.

Mechanism

Every routine runs the same loop: read X, rank, deliver.

Public posts need nothing. Your timeline and bookmarks come in through the X connector. A routine fires on a cron schedule or an alert, Zosia summarizes and ranks, and the result goes to chat or email.
