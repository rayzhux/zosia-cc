§ 04 · Hybrid search stack

The 9:20 GST run, end-to-end.

Daily flow with explicit tool-per-step and ~30 min total wall time. Kimi-cn cost is trivial — ~¥14/month, ≈ $2 USD at 12 daily searches + 20 on Sunday's deep sweep.

  1. 06:25 GST

    RSS sweep · blogwatcher

    Pull the few sources that publish RSS: The National Business, Arabian Business, CnEVPost, Pandaily, Khaleej Times, Gulf News. Store raw title / url / source / time.

  2. 06:35 GST

    Exa English / Gulf sweep

    6-8 Exa queries over prior 36h, focused on Gulf-side wires + business press: China UAE investment agreement, Chinese company Saudi manufacturing, PIF China technology, WAM China investment, Zawya Chinese company UAE, AETOSWire China Saudi.

  3. 06:45 GST

    Kimi-cn native CN sweep

    10-14 Kimi searches via the kimi-cn skill (scripts/kimi "..."). Rotate from the 55-query taxonomy, prioritizing: 出海中东, 沙特/阿联酋建厂, 中东主权基金投资中国, plus daily company watchlist + sector watchlist.

  4. 06:58 GST

    Filing check

    For any named listed-company capex / JV / convertible / plant signal: check SSE / SZSE / HKEXnews / company IR. Filings outrank media every time. Material announcements get bumped to confirmed immediately.

  5. 07:05 GST

    Bilingual normalization

    Normalize every entity to its bilingual form: BYD/比亚迪, NIO/蔚来, WeRide/文远知行, Lenovo/联想, PIF/沙特公共投资基金, Mubadala/穆巴达拉, ADQ. Prevents same-deal duplication across source legs.

  6. 07:08 GST

    Cluster + dedup

    Cluster candidates by {CN company × Gulf counterparty × country × signal_type} with a 14-day window. Merge Chinese-source + Gulf-source items into one bilingual story. Keep the earliest source AND the strongest source separately (the metadata matters for confidence labeling).

  7. 07:12 GST

    Editorial filter (5Q gate)

    Apply the five filter questions. Must hit ≥3. Kill anything that's PR exhaust, generic geopolitics, or showroom-only auto news without a fleet/distributor/assembly hook.

  8. 07:15 GST

    Confidence label + score

    Tag each survivor: confirmed (filing or Gulf-official + CN-source) · reported (single credible source) · watch (social/analyst-only). Score by category × proof level. Take the top ≤5.

  9. 09:20 GST

    Discord post (or skip)

    If ≥2 qualified items: Components v2 post to #🧠-intel. If <2: log silently, no post — quiet days stay quiet. Update dedup ledger.

● Confirmed

Official filing or Gulf wire + CN company / filing. Two-sided cross-confirmation.

● Reported

One credible source (CN or Gulf) without counterparty confirmation. Ships, but tagged.

● Watch

Social / analyst chatter only. Doesn't ship unless strategically important enough to flag.

§ 05 · PR-channel cheat sheet

Where deals actually break.

The most useful section in this dossier. If a Chinese company is announcing GCC entry — or a Gulf entity is announcing China-linked capital — here's the order in which it surfaces. Real recent examples included so the cron knows what to weight.

CN company → GCC entry · what hits first
1
Corporate PR / IR / exchange filing

If listed or material: company newsroom + SSE/SZSE/HKEX filing. This is the source. Media is downstream.

Example — Lenovo–Alat (PIF) was announced via Lenovo's newsroom 2024-05-29; Xinhua / Arab News / Reuters amplified afterward. The $2B convertible + Riyadh manufacturing hub originated in HKEX disclosure.
2
CN financial flash + 出海 verticals

财联社, 证券时报, 第一财经 catch filings within minutes. 36氪出海 / EqualOcean / 亿欧出海 package as globalization narrative within hours.

Example — 36氪 highlighted Lenovo's PIF/Alat investment + Riyadh HQ angle; Yicai covered the GCL/TCL/Jinko Saudi/UAE solar push.
3
Sector verticals when it's technical

For AI / AV / EV / battery: 量子位, 智东西, 机器之心, 盖世汽车, 第一电动, 汽车之家 often have wedge-specific detail before generalist press notices.

Example — 量子位 packaged WeRide × Uber Abu Dhabi/Dubai/Riyadh robotaxi expansion in native Chinese before English coverage caught the GCC angle.
4
Chinese gov portals when policy-led

MOFCOM 走出去 + 中国一带一路网 repost investment-promotion events such as "投资中国" Middle East sessions with named Chinese companies and Gulf counterparts. CCPIT does the same for delegations.

5
Social / corporate accounts (soft launch)

Weibo + WeChat 公众号 often precede formal English coverage for store openings, pilot licenses, local hiring, conference demos. Treat as reported, not confirmed.

Gulf entity → China-linked capital/demand · what hits first
1
Official wire or ministry PR

WAM (UAE), SPA (Saudi), QNA (Qatar), KUNA (Kuwait), BNA (Bahrain) carry the official MOU / agreement first. The National + Zawya follow.

Example — WAM carried the UAE Ministry of Investment × Jereh Group 杰瑞股份 clean-energy / industrial platform deal; Zawya and The National picked it up after.
2
SWF / strategic-platform newsroom

PIF / Alat, Mubadala, ADQ, CYVN, MGX, QIA, Aramco Ventures / Prosperity7 publish capital announcements before media gets briefed. These newsrooms are the source-of-truth for sovereign-led deals.

Example — Lenovo–Alat $2B convertible + Riyadh hub was a textbook PIF-platform signal; Alat newsroom + PIF announcement preceded amplification.
3
Free-zone newsroom

DMCC / JAFZA / KEZAD / ADGM / RAKEZ / QFZ announce delegations, setup incentives, and Chinese registrations before dailies notice. RAKEZ's China city delegation tour and ADGM × Shenzhen Futian MoU are pipeline signals worth catching.

4
AETOSWire / Zawya PR

Many MENA corporate announcements are paid-distribution first — especially market entry, partnerships, fund launches, and conference-timed MOUs. AETOSWire is the dominant regional wire; Zawya PR aggregates.

5
Regional dailies validate importance

The National, Arab News, Khaleej Times, Arabian Business, Argaam, AGBI help judge whether an item matters locally or is just PR exhaust. If they don't pick it up within 48h, it usually isn't the signal.

Rule of thumb

Chinese-source-only = reported, early signal. Gulf official + Chinese company / filing = confirmed. Cross-confirmation is the strongest filter we have.

§ 06 · Editorial filter

Five questions, plus one v0.2 modifier.

Same five questions from v0.1 — they were sound. v0.2 adds one rule on source confidence so MOU theater doesn't clog the channel.

  1. Does it map to the two-ecosystem thesis?

    China supply / tech / manufacturing meeting Gulf capital / demand / regulatory sandbox. If you can't draw the corridor in one sentence, kill it.

  2. Is there a named, actionable actor?

    Company, fund, ministry, executive, chamber, free zone, distributor — someone Raymond could know, partner with, sell to, or track.

  3. Is it in or near a Dhow / WAG lane?

    Auto, EV, AV, fleet/logistics, AI infra, robotics, industrial localization, venture-studio wedge ideas. Adjacent gets a flag, not a kill.

  4. Is this pipeline or proof?

    Signed agreement, license, money committed, plant location, first orders — beats vague intent or "studying cooperation."

  5. What changed since yesterday?

    New capital number, license, commercial launch, facility location, procurement volume, named counterpart.

v0.2 modifier · Source confidence matters

A low-detail MOU from a ministry can ship only if it creates a named pipeline. A social-only rumor cannot ship unless flagged as watch, not news. Cross-confirmation between Chinese source + Gulf source bumps confidence, not just count.
